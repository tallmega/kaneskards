# Kane's Kards Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Kane's Kards is an offline reading-practice app for children. We designed it to work without accounts, advertising, analytics, or network services.

## Information we collect

Kane's Kards does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising ID, or any other sensitive permission.

The chosen practice-round size is saved only on the device so the app can remember that setting. This setting is not sent anywhere and is removed when the app's data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for young readers. Because it does not collect or transmit personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
